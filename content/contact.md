We are based in Kansas City, Missouri. Our volunteers bring portable labs to schools, conferences, maker spaces and community events across the Midwest.

## Workshop requests

Tell us the event date, location and expected number of participants. [Email the workshop team](mailto:workshops@fellowshipoftheflux.com) or [compare workshop formats](/services/).

## Volunteer coordination

Interested in teaching, equipment or event support? [Explore volunteer roles](/get-involved/) or [email the volunteer coordinator](mailto:volunteers@fellowshipoftheflux.com).

## General inquiries

Reach us at [info@fellowshipoftheflux.com](mailto:info@fellowshipoftheflux.com). You can also find the Fellowship on [GitHub](https://github.com/fellowshipoftheflux) and [Twitter](https://twitter.com/fluxfellowship).
