<section class="manual-section" aria-labelledby="plan-for-the-work">
<h2 id="plan-for-the-work">Plan for the work</h2>
<p>Choose a setup around the projects, people and venue. Check the station maker's instructions and the venue's electrical, ventilation and safety requirements before use.</p>
<div class="fact-grid">
<article><h3>People and projects</h3><p>List the expected group size, project steps and level of direct instruction. These choices determine the number and type of stations.</p></article>
<article><h3>Room and utilities</h3><p>Check table space, outlets, cable paths, lighting, ventilation, storage and accessible circulation with the venue.</p></article>
<article><h3>Transport and setup</h3><p>Plan how equipment will be packed, labeled, checked before use and returned to storage after the activity.</p></article>
</div>
</section>

<section class="manual-section" aria-labelledby="equipment-recommendations">
<h2 id="equipment-recommendations">Equipment checklist</h2>
<div class="fact-grid">
<article><h3>Workstations</h3><ul><li>Soldering stations that suit the planned work</li><li>Compatible handpieces, stands and replaceable tips</li><li>Stable, heat-resistant work surfaces</li><li>Power distribution appropriate for the room</li></ul></article>
<article><h3>Tools and materials</h3><ul><li>Cutters, pliers and wire preparation tools</li><li>Project parts and boards</li><li>Solder and flux selected for the activity</li><li>Cleaning and maintenance supplies</li></ul></article>
<article><h3>Safety and access</h3><ul><li>Personal protective equipment selected for the work</li><li>Ventilation reviewed for the materials and venue</li><li>Clear first-aid and emergency procedures</li><li>Work areas arranged for safe, accessible movement</li></ul></article>
<article><h3>Storage and inventory</h3><ul><li>Protective cases for transport</li><li>Clearly labeled tool and parts storage</li><li>A packing and return checklist</li><li>A maintenance and replacement-parts log</li></ul></article>
</div>
</section>

<section class="manual-section" aria-labelledby="planning-checklist">
<h2 id="planning-checklist">Before the first session</h2>
<ol>
<li>Confirm the project instructions, parts and tools.</li>
<li>Review the venue's electrical and safety requirements with the responsible person.</li>
<li>Set up and test the equipment in the planned room.</li>
<li>Check that the layout, signage and instructions work for the people attending.</li>
<li>Record what needs to be packed, replenished or maintained afterward.</li>
</ol>
<p>For soldering hazards and personal protective equipment, read the <a href="/learn/guides/soldering-safety/">soldering safety guide</a> and have the guidance reviewed for your setting.</p>
</section>

<section class="manual-section" aria-labelledby="cost-planning">
<h2 id="cost-planning">Estimate costs from current sources</h2>
<p>Equipment prices, availability and specifications change. Build a local estimate from current manufacturer or authorized-supplier listings for the station, consumables, protective equipment, storage, maintenance and replacement parts. Confirm that each item is compatible with your work and venue.</p>
</section>
